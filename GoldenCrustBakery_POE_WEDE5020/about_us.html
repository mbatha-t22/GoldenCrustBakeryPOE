Our story

About Golden Crusts Bakery

A family-run bakery bringing fresh, affordable treats to Pretoria and the surrounding local community.

Our history

Golden Crusts Bakery opened its doors in Pretoria, Gauteng, in 2023. Founded by Portia Khumalo, the bakery provides fresh bread, cakes, cupcakes, pastries and personalised celebration cakes.

Our mission

Our mission is to provide delicious treats that are fresh, tasty and affordable while creating a friendly and welcoming customer experience.

Our vision

We aim to become a trusted go-to bakery in Gauteng by reaching more customers and making our baked goods easier to discover online.
